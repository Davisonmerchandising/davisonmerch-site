    Questions from schools and teams

    What is the minimum order?

    60 pairs per design. Most teams order 100 or more so there are enough for players, coaches and fans.

    When should we order?

    Plan 6–8 weeks before you need them. That covers your proof, 3–4 weeks of production and shipping.

    Can we use our school logo or mascot?

    Yes, when your school or booster club has permission to use it. Many school logos are trademarks, so check with your athletic department if you’re not sure.

    How do socks work as a fundraiser?

    You buy at our per-pair price and sell to families and fans at your own price, often $12–20 a pair. We can pack each pair with a hang tag for easy selling.

    Which style is best for sports?

    Performance socks have cushioned soles and breathable zones for game day. Premium cotton is great for spirit wear and fundraisers.

    Can you create the design for us?

    Yes. Send your logo, colors and an idea, and our designers will build the sock and send a free mockup.

    What sizes are available?

    Tell us your team’s sizes in your quote request and we’ll confirm the size options for your style.

    How do we pay?

    Pay by card online once you approve your proof. Contact us if your school needs to pay with a purchase order.
