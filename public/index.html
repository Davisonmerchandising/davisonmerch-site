    FAQs

    What is the minimum order quantity?

    60 pairs per style.

    How much do custom socks cost?

    Per pair, per style. Use the pricing calculator for an estimate. Your quote confirms the final price.

    How long does production take?

    3–4 weeks after proof approval, for every sock style.

    Will I see my design before production starts?

    Yes. You approve a digital proof and tech pack before anything is made.

    What if I don’t have a design?

    Ask for a quote anyway. Our designers will build one with you.

    What file types can I send?

    AI, PDF, EPS, SVG, PNG or JPG, up to 5 MB. Larger files can be emailed to sales@davisonmerch.com.

    What sizes are available?

    Sublimation and DTG socks come in US Mens 9–13. Ask about other sizes.

    Do you offer branded packaging?

    Yes. Custom or stock printed packaging.
