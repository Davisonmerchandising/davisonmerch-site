Work with us

Any questions?

Distributor questions, answered below. For anything else, email us.

sales@davisonmerch.com
Who can become a distributor partner?

Promotional products distributors, agencies and resellers. Tell us about your business in the quote form and a sales rep will follow up.

What is the minimum order?

60 pairs per style.

How long do orders take?

3–4 weeks after proof approval, for every sock style.

Do you help with design?

Yes. Send your client’s art, or come to us without a design. We build the drawings and tech pack, and you approve a digital proof before production.

Can socks arrive in branded packaging?

Yes. Custom or stock printed packaging.

How does distributor pricing work?

Per pair, per style, and it drops as quantity goes up. Your sales rep will price your program.
