Questions

What is the minimum order?

60 pairs per style.

How much do custom socks cost?

Premium cotton crew socks run from $9.54 a pair at 60 pairs down to $5.95 a pair at 2,000+. Performance and merino styles cost a little more. Your quote confirms the final price.

How long does production take?

3–4 weeks after you approve your proof, then free shipping.

Will I see my design before you make it?

Yes. You approve a free digital proof and tech pack before anything is knit.

I don’t have a design. Can you help?

Yes. Send your logo or an idea and our designers will build the sock with you.

What files can I send?

AI, PDF, EPS, SVG, PNG or JPG. A phone photo of a sketch is fine to start.

What sizes are available?

Sublimation and DTG socks come in US Mens 9–13. Ask about other sizes for knit styles.

Do you offer packaging?

Yes. Custom or stock printed packaging and retail-ready finishing.
