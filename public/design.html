The details

Know what’s included.

One brand. One agreed brief.

Graphics, colorways and mockups

Every graphic is original and refined through your feedback rounds before delivery. Each concept is shown in 3 colorways on 3 sock mockups, so you get 3, 9, 15 or 30 mockups depending on your package.

Feedback and final artwork

Feedback is gathered into one set per round across all graphics. A new brief needs an updated scope. You receive editable and PDF artwork, matching sock mockups, and color and placement guidance. File formats are confirmed before work begins.

Additional design work

Work beyond your package, including extra feedback rounds, is $150 per hour. We send an estimate for your approval first.

Payment and production

Single Graphic is paid up front. Multi-graphic packages are paid 50% to start and 50% at final design approval, before final artwork is delivered. Prices are in USD. Socks and physical samples are billed separately, and knitting specs are confirmed in your production quote.
